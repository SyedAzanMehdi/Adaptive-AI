# Part A — Student Guide

## A1. Getting Started

### A1.1 Create your account

1. Open the platform and click **Register**.
2. Enter your name, email, and a strong password.
3. Confirm your email if prompted. You are registered as a **Student**.

### A1.2 Sign in

1. Click **Login** and enter your credentials.
2. Your session is secured with an issuer-bound JWT token — you stay signed in until it expires, then you will be asked to log in again.

## A2. The Diagnostic Assessment

When you first join, the platform runs an **intelligent diagnostic** to learn exactly where you are strong and weak.

1. From your dashboard, click **Start Diagnostic**.
2. You will receive **dynamic code-reading and problem-solving questions** generated by AI — they adapt to every answer you give.
   - Answer well → questions get harder and probe deeper areas.
   - Struggle → the system eases difficulty and explores the weak spot.
3. Answer each question and submit. There is a time-friendly pause: you can **pause and resume** within 7 days.
4. When complete, the platform builds your **Capability Matrix** — a personal map of strengths and weaknesses across areas like syntax, OOP, data structures, and debugging.

**Tips**
- Answer honestly — the goal is an accurate map, not a high score.
- Take breaks; your progress is saved automatically.

## A3. Learning Lessons (Adaptive Delivery)

1. Open a lesson from your curriculum.
2. If the platform detects you're struggling with a concept (e.g., Object-Oriented Programming), it **rewrites the lesson for you automatically**:
   - **Beginner** → real-world, physical analogies.
   - **Intermediate** → memory and execution diagrams, step-by-step traces.
   - **Advanced** → deeper internals and design discussions.
3. The adapted lesson teaches the **same objectives** — just in the style that fits you best.
4. If the AI is momentarily slow, you'll see the standard lesson right away; the adaptation never blocks you.

## A4. Writing and Submitting Code

1. Open an exercise; the **in-browser code editor** loads.
2. Write your solution and click **Submit**.
3. The AI mentor evaluates your code on **four dimensions**:
   - **Correctness** — does it work?
   - **Style** — is it clean and readable?
   - **Edge cases** — does it handle unusual inputs?
   - **Optimization** — is it efficient?
4. You receive **constructive, tiered feedback** matched to your level:
   - Beginners get guided hints and encouragement.
   - Intermediate learners get targeted corrections tied to concepts.
   - Advanced learners get design and optimization critiques.
5. Your feedback is saved — view your **submission history** anytime.
6. Each submission updates your Capability Matrix, so future lessons adapt to your real progress.

**Note:** There is a fair-use limit on rapid submissions. If you hit it, wait for the retry timer shown on screen.

## A5. Tracking Your Mastery

- Your dashboard shows the **Capability Matrix** and per-concept mastery.
- Weak areas are highlighted with suggested lessons and exercises.
- Progress updates automatically after assessments and code submissions.

## A6. Ask AI (Chat)

1. Open **Ask AI** in the top navigation.
2. Type any question — programming concepts, algorithms, debugging, study strategies, careers, or platform help — and press **Send** (or Enter).
3. Replies arrive in seconds with a topic tag; suggestion chips help you start.
4. Your full conversation is saved privately — use **Clear** to reset it.

**Tips**
- Ask follow-ups: the mentor remembers the last part of your conversation.
- Chat is rate-limited for fairness; if you hit the limit, wait a few minutes.

## A7. Memory Twin™ (Premium)

Your Memory Twin models how fast you forget each skill, based on your real practice history.

1. Open **Memory Twin** in the navigation (requires Adaptive+).
2. Read the **14-day retention forecast**: the curve shows your predicted recall if you stop practicing; the red dashed line marks the 50% danger threshold.
3. Check each domain card for current recall % and memory stability (days).
4. When a domain shows "Fades below 50% in N days", start a **Rescue Review**: two quick questions that reinforce the memory and measurably increase its stability.

## A8. Struggle DNA™ (Premium)

1. Open **Struggle DNA** in the navigation.
2. Free plan: you see your **archetype teaser** (e.g., Edge-Case Blind).
3. Adaptive+: you see the full report — a 4-axis radar (Resilience, Depth Tolerance, Edge Awareness, Craft) plus numbered **countermeasures** tailored to your archetype.

## A9. Adaptive+ Subscription

1. Open the **Premium** page (click your plan badge in the top bar).
2. Compare **Explorer (Free)** with **Adaptive+**.
3. Pick your regional price — the upgrade page offers purchasing-power-parity points (Pakistan ₨999 default, India ₹299, Bangladesh ৳349, Nigeria ₦2900, Egypt E£149, Indonesia Rp49,000, Brazil R$24.90, US $9.99). Your choice is remembered on this device.
4. Click **Subscribe now** — your badge flips to Adaptive+ and Memory Twin, Rescue Reviews, the full Struggle DNA report, and Career Autopilot unlock immediately.
5. Billing is simulated in this build; a payment provider integrates at the same endpoint in production.

## A10. Domain Compass™

1. Open **Compass** in the navigation.
2. Browse all **64 computing domains** as cards, or narrow the grid with a field filter (AI/ML, cybersecurity, quantum, cloud/DevOps, data, web, mobile, IoT/robotics, XR, blockchain, bioinformatics, core foundations).
3. Click any domain to open its detail view: a full description, its 0–100 "viral potential" score, and a **4-stage study path** (Foundations → Core Skills → Applied → Mastery) — each stage tells you what to study and the milestone that proves you got there.
4. Every domain sits inside a field with a 2026–2036 demand forecast and a plain-language outlook on why it will (or won't) explode by 2036.
5. Domains that map to platform competencies deep-link straight into the matching filtered lesson catalog.

## A11. PathFinder™ Study Planner

1. Open **Planner** in the navigation.
2. If you've completed the diagnostic, PathFinder builds a personalized 7-day schedule — weakest skills first, spaced so the most fragile skill recurs most often, each focus day pairing a lesson with a mentored exercise.
3. Day 7 re-baselines: retake the diagnostic and the next week re-plans itself automatically.
4. New students get a balanced rotation with one click to personalize later.

## A12. Career Autopilot™ (Premium)

1. Open **Autopilot** in the navigation (requires Adaptive+).
2. Paste any job description and run the analysis.
3. Review the extracted skills diffed against your Capability Matrix (strong / developing / gap / unmeasured), your importance-weighted hire-readiness score, and the generated 90-day plan across three phases.
4. Your latest plan is saved and reloads on return visits.

## A13. System Design Dojo™

1. Open **Design Dojo** in the navigation.
2. Study the 5-step framework: **Clarify → Estimate → Model → Architect → Scale**.
3. Choose one of six challenges (URL shortener, rate limiter, chat system, news feed, ride matching, video streaming) and write your full design (at least 80 characters of substance).
4. Submit to receive a 4-axis critique — Requirements clarity, Estimation, Data modeling, Scalability — with scores, strengths, gaps, and next steps. Your history is saved per challenge.

## A14. Skill Passport™

1. Open **Passport** in the navigation.
2. Your passport compiles your measured capability — per-domain mastery, code submissions, design critiques, and Autopilot readiness — under a stable passport ID.
3. **Download passport JSON** for applications, **Copy to clipboard**, or **Copy as resume** for a plain-text version you can paste anywhere.
4. Free for all plans; mastery scores refresh live as you learn.

## A15. Scholarship Radar™

1. Open **Scholarships** in the navigation.
2. Browse 15 curated fully-funded international scholarships, each with a live deadline countdown that rolls to the next open cycle automatically.
3. Filter by degree level, destination country, and field of study — results re-rank by match score, prioritizing full funding and programmes with strong Pakistan track records.
4. Free for all plans. Always confirm details on the official programme site before applying.

## A16. Freelance Launchpad™

1. Open **Freelance** in the navigation.
2. Optionally type a focus area (e.g., "backend automation scripts").
3. Click **Generate my freelance profile** — the Launchpad reads your live Capability Matrix and drafts a headline, niche, skills, positioning, 2–3 starter gigs with price bands, and a realistic hourly rate. It never claims skills your matrix doesn't support.
4. **Copy profile** to paste into Fiverr/Upwork/LinkedIn. Regenerate any time — the profile improves as your matrix improves.

**Dual-language helper.** The dashboard includes an Urdu bilingual glossary (Urdu script + Roman transliteration + plain meaning) with live search — use it any time a term feels unfamiliar.

## A17. Student FAQ

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can I retake the diagnostic? | Yes — the dashboard button becomes **Retake Diagnostic** once complete; your matrix re-baselining also re-plans your PathFinder week. |
| Can other students see my data? | No. Your profile, matrix, submissions, feedback, and chat history are private to you. |
| Why did my lesson change? | The platform detected a struggle and rewrote it in your preferred style — this is the core feature working as intended. |
| Feedback looks wrong — what now? | Resubmit a revised solution, or contact an Admin who can review the feedback record. |
| Why do I get a 402 error? | That feature requires Adaptive+ Premium — visit the Premium page to subscribe. |
| Does it work on phones and tablets? | Yes. The app is fully responsive — a hamburger menu on phones, a slide-in admin sidebar, and safe-area support for notched devices. |

# Part B — Admin Guide

## B1. Accessing the Admin Dashboard

1. Sign in with an admin-provisioned account (admins are created via seeding or by another admin — never self-registered).
2. Your JWT carries the `admin` role, unlocking the **Admin Dashboard**. Student tokens are automatically rejected from these pages (HTTP 403).

## B2. Managing Users

1. Navigate to **Admin → Users**.
2. You can:
   - **Create** student or admin accounts.
   - **Suspend / reactivate** accounts.
   - **Change roles** (student ↔ admin) — this action is audit-logged.
   - **Grant / revoke Adaptive+ Premium** (Grant ★ / Revoke ★) — also audit-logged.
3. Search and filter by email, role, plan, or status.

## B3. Curriculum Settings

1. Navigate to **Admin → Curriculum**.
2. Configure:
   - Competency domains covered by diagnostics.
   - Mastery thresholds that trigger lesson adaptation (default 0.6).
   - Diagnostic length / confidence targets.
   - Difficulty bands for beginner / intermediate / advanced tiers.
3. Save changes — they apply to subsequent diagnostics and adaptations.

## B4. Analytics & Monitoring

1. Navigate to **Admin → Analytics**.
2. Monitor:
   - Diagnostic completion rates and average scores.
   - Per-concept mastery distribution across students.
   - AI usage: call volume, latency, cache hit rate, fallback rate.
   - Submission volume and average feedback scores.
3. Use these to tune curriculum thresholds and AI budgets.

## B5. Audit Log & Security

1. Navigate to **Admin → Audit Log**.
2. Review: admin actions (user changes, curriculum edits) and security events (RBAC denials, token tampering, cross-user access attempts).
3. Investigate any spike in security events immediately.

**Admin security rules**
- Never share admin credentials.
- Revoke (suspend) accounts immediately on offboarding.
- Privileged actions are always logged — act accordingly.

## B6. Admin FAQ

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can a student see admin pages? | No — RBAC middleware returns 403 for any student token on admin routes. |
| Can I read a student's private data? | Admins can view student records for support/analytics; all such access is scoped and logged. |
| The AI is slow — what happens? | Students automatically receive cached/canonical content; check Analytics → fallback rate. |
| How do I control AI cost? | Tune rate limits, cache TTLs, and diagnostic length in Curriculum settings. |
